The plan

I bring the mat and the music, you (or we) bring the snacks and drinks, and we eat, chat, laugh, and read side by side while the tunes play, or the birds sing, or, in true LA style, the cars rumble past haha.

Abhi brings

The picnic mat, a little speaker, and the playlist below.

Alice brings

Snacks and drinks, whatever you're in the mood for. Though bringing yourself alone would qualify as a "snack" haha.

We both bring

A book each, sunglasses, sunscreen, and a light layer in case we stay for sunset.
